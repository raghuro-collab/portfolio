About Me

The artist
behind the cut

Hi, I'm Raghu M — a short form video editor based in Karnataka, India. I specialise in transforming raw, unpolished footage into scroll-stopping content that resonates with audiences and drives real engagement.

With a deep understanding of visual rhythm, pacing, and storytelling, I work across Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and TikTok — bringing a cinematic sensibility to every project, no matter how brief the format.

Whether it's a brand campaign, a travel vlog, or a personal highlight reel — I approach every edit with the same level of precision and creative intention.
